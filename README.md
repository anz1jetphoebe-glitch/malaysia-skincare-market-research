# malaysia-skincare-market-research
Market research and business development analysis of independent Malaysian skincare brands.

# Malaysia Independent Skincare Brands
## Market Research & Business Development

### 1. Project Overview

This project explores independent skincare brands
in Malaysia and evaluates their potential for
international beauty exhibitions.

### 2. Project Objectives

- Identify 20 independent Malaysian skincare brands.
- Research their products and brand positioning.
- Collect publicly available business information.
- Evaluate their international exhibition potential.
- Develop a B2B exhibitor acquisition strategy.

### 3. Research Scope

Target Market: Malaysia

Industry: Beauty & Skincare

Target Companies: Independent and emerging local brands

Research Focus:
- Brand background
- Product categories
- Target customers
- Official website
- Public business contact channels
- International business potential

### 4. Research Methodology

Information will be collected from publicly
available company websites, brand social media,
industry publications and other reliable sources.

Each brand will be evaluated using consistent
research criteria.

### 5. Expected Deliverables

- Brand database (20 companies)
- Market overview
- Competitor analysis
- Exhibitor potential assessment
- Business development recommendations

### 6. Skills Demonstrated

- International Market Research
- B2B Business Development
- Competitive Analysis
- Data Collection
- Business Communication
- Cross-border Market Research
